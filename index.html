Club Rules & Regulations 👇🏻

🪧 Rule No: 01
Do not post any unwanted or unrelated content in the group. Only biker-related posts are allowed.

🪧 Rule No: 02
Club admins are not responsible for any buying or selling transactions made in the group or during rides.

🪧 Rule No: 03
Overtaking fellow riders during the ride is strictly not allowed and will not be accepted.

🪧 Rule No: 04
Team members should not change the club name or profile picture.

🪧 Rule No: 05
👉 A Monthly contribution of ₹50/- is mandatory for all team members 👈
👉 This fee helps us manage administration, event coordination, and safety support. 💰 Payment Period: 1st to 15th of every month.

🪧 Rule No: 06
Line discipline must be strictly followed by every rider during the ride.

🪧 Rule No: 07
Dear riders, please respect all fellow riders and admins.

🪧 Rule No: 08
No scope for racing during rides or any kind of caste or gender-based talk within the team.

🪧 Rule No: 09
Following the instructions of Captains, Marshals, and Sweepers is mandatory.

🪧 Rule No: 10
During the ride, the Captain, Marshals, and Sweepers will guide for everyone’s safety. Safety is our top priority.

🪧 Rule No: 11
Admins have the right to terminate any member from the group at any time if they fail to follow the team’s rules and regulations.

🪧 Rule No: 12
Every member must attend a minimum of 2 rides every 6 months. Otherwise, they will be removed from the team.

🪧 Rule No: 13
During rides, only beverages like coffee or tea are sponsored by RRC.

🪧 Rule No: 14
Once registration is done ✅, the registration fee is non-refundable.

🪧 Rule No: 15
Before the ride flag-off, clear instructions and hand signal gestures will be shared and explained. Every rider must follow them during the ride.

🪧 Rule No: 16
No scope for misunderstandings, politics, or friction within the club during or after rides. If noticed by admins, immediate termination will follow.

🪧 Rule No: 17
No political agendas — national or local — are allowed within the club or during rides. Such actions are strictly prohibited.

🪧 Rule No: 18
During rides, maintaining proper timing and discipline is mandatory.

🪧 Rule No: 19
For any unwanted or unpleasant situations during rides or within the club, admins will not be held responsible.

🪧 Rule No: 20
No one should interfere with individual safety, integrity, privacy, or individuality. Respect everyone equally.

🪧 Rule No: 21
Let’s make every ride filled with joy, good memories, and positive vibes. A perfect getaway from our busy lives!

🪧 Rule No: 22
If any rider joins another club, please choose one club and leave the other. Support only one club to keep it strong and united. 🏍️ Being in multiple clubs creates confusion and affects team spirit. Please respect the rules and stay loyal to one family. Otherwise, RRC will remove you from the club.

🍀🏍️💨🎉🎊 Good Luck & Safe Rides!
